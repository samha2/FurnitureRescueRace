    Give Furniture a New Life

    Collect reused boards, legs and cushions, repair them for free at the workshop, then deliver the current order. Choose between buying and repairing, and see how your choices affect the outcome.

    • Buy a healthy new part: 8 coins and +20 impact.
    • Discard a part: +10 impact. Repairs are free and add no impact.
    • Each delivery earns 15 points and 8 coins, plus 5 bonus points if every part is reused.
    • In Challenge mode, you lose when time runs out or impact reaches 100.
    Start with 24 coins and space for 6 parts. Move with arrow keys or WASD. Stand on a part or station and press E. The on-screen movement buttons support touch. At the workshop, select parts with keys 1–6 and press R to repair, or use the mouse.

    🔧 Free Repair Workshop

    Select a part from your bag and repair it to 100% condition.

    1. Select a damaged part from your bag. Its condition will be below 100%.
    2. Click “Repair Part for Free.”
    3. Wait until the part reaches 100%. Repeat for each damaged part, then click “Back to Map.”

    Repair one part at a time. Parts already at 100% do not need repair.

    🛒 New Parts Store

    A new part is in 100% condition, costs 8 coins and adds 20 impact points. Using it removes the +5 reuse bonus for that order.

    🗑️ Trash Bin

    Select a part to discard. Each discarded part adds 10 impact points. You can close this window and keep the part for repair instead.
